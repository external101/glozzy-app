Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you use Glozzy, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information (name, email address)
  • Social media account data you connect to the Service
  • Campaign and analytics data generated through your use of the Service
  • Usage data (how you interact with the Service)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the Service
  • Display campaign analytics and insights
  • Communicate with you about your account
  • Ensure the security of the Service

3. Data from Third-Party Platforms

When you connect your social media accounts (such as TikTok), we access certain data through their APIs in accordance with their developer terms. This data is used solely to provide you with analytics and campaign management features. We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect the rights and safety of Glozzy and its users

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can also disconnect your social media accounts and revoke our access to your data.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the Service or via email.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@glozzy.app.
